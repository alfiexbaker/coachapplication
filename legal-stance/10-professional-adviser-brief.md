# Professional adviser brief

## Purpose

Use this brief to obtain scoped, written advice rather than paying professionals to rediscover the product. Provide the complete pack, current checkout mock-ups, Supplier onboarding, privacy/data map and payment-provider proposed configuration.

Clubroom's preferred launch position is a football-specific software platform and managed marketplace for paid, in-person coaching in England. The identified coach/business or club supplies the coaching. The adult parent/guardian books; the child participates. Clubroom is principal for its own platform/SaaS and intends to act as a disclosed Supplier-side booking agent for the coaching contract. It will use cards/wallets through a regulated marketplace PSP, not Direct Debit, and will allow multiple sessions/children in one payment only for one legal Supplier.

## Commercial/consumer marketplace solicitor

Please provide a written opinion and drafting plan addressing:

1. Whether the proposed conduct supports disclosed Supplier-side agency rather than Clubroom being principal for coaching.
2. Required pre-contract supplier identity, total price, cancellation/refund and complaint disclosures.
3. The Consumer Contracts Regulations specific-date leisure exemption and treatment of open/recurring programmes.
4. Consumer Rights Act fairness and remedies.
5. Supplier agreement, platform/family terms and organisation SaaS terms.
6. Reasonable consumer/B2B caps, statutory carve-outs, Supplier warranties and indemnities.
7. Reviews, rankings and credential statements under current unfair-commercial-practices rules.
8. Independent contractor/worker/employment risks created by platform controls.
9. England-only governing law/jurisdiction and what changes for other UK nations.
10. Accessibility/equality duties and reasonable-adjustment process.

Ask counsel to provide a red/amber/green checkout and contract architecture, not only mark-up.

## Payments/FCA counsel

Please confirm:

1. Whether Clubroom's proposed Supplier-only appointment fits the commercial-agent exclusion.
2. Whether the exact Stripe Connect charge, fee, refund, payout and negative-balance configuration creates regulated payment activity for Clubroom.
3. What Clubroom may say and do about settlement, reserves, refunds and disputes.
4. Whether any planned multi-Supplier grouped cart changes the analysis.
5. Whether provider onboarding/KYC and agreements are sufficient or Clubroom needs its own due diligence.
6. What contract and interface wording is needed to keep Clubroom from appearing to hold funds or offer escrow.

Request an opinion against a money-flow diagram and actual provider terms.

## Accountant/tax adviser

Please confirm in writing:

1. Principal versus agent accounting and whether Clubroom revenue is commission/SaaS or gross coaching receipts.
2. VAT treatment of Clubroom fees and Supplier coaching, registration forecast and inclusive/exclusive pricing.
3. Receipt, invoice, credit-note and Supplier-statement requirements.
4. Refunds, discounts, bad debt, reserves and timing of revenue.
5. Corporation tax, founder remuneration/loan and bookkeeping controls.
6. Digital-platform seller reporting, due-diligence fields, reportable amounts and filing timetable.
7. Whether the proposed ledger is adequate for audit and tax.

Provide scenarios for VAT-unregistered and VAT-registered Suppliers and Clubroom.

## Privacy/data counsel or experienced DPO

Please approve or correct:

1. The operation-by-operation controller/processor/joint-controller matrix.
2. Child/family authority, multiple guardians, court restrictions, competent teens and unsafe-guardian escalation.
3. Article 6/9/10 lawful bases for identity, health, safeguarding and DBS.
4. DPIA, Children's Code, Appropriate Policy Document and DPO-threshold decision.
5. Data-sharing, Article 26/28 and international-transfer terms.
6. Medical/safeguarding projection and offline access.
7. Retention for medical, safeguarding, messages, media, injuries, verification, analytics and audit.
8. Rights, account deletion, complaints, marketing/PECR and breach operations.
9. Apple privacy labels/manifests consistency with actual SDK/data flows.

## Safeguarding specialist

Please review:

1. Supplier eligibility/DBS and safer-recruitment standard for each delivery model.
2. Adult-child communications and guardian-centred design.
3. Referral and information-sharing thresholds.
4. Unsafe guardian, allegations against coaches, disclosure confidentiality and evidence preservation.
5. Missing child, late collection, medical emergency and venue incident procedures.
6. Named lead, external cover, response times, training and audit.
7. Claims Clubroom may make about verification and safeguarding.

## Insurance broker

Please quote and explain exclusions for technology E&O/PI, cyber/privacy, public/product liability, D&O, crime/social engineering, media/UGC, safeguarding/abuse defence and employers' liability when applicable. Confirm whether contractual indemnities, payment losses, child data and marketplace activities are covered.

## Apple/App Review pre-submission

Use Apple's current official channels and submission notes to validate:

- external card/wallet payment for in-person coaching;
- the absence of paid remote one-to-many services and family digital subscriptions;
- any separately contracted organisation SaaS access;
- account deletion, Sign in with Apple position, privacy labels/manifests and child/UGC controls;
- review demo accounts, backend availability and explanation of physical-service checkout.

Apple does not normally give a binding advance legal opinion for every feature. Treat successful review as platform approval for that submitted build, not an endorsement of Clubroom or a permanent guarantee against future policy change.

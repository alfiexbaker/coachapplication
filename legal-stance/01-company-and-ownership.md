# Company and Ownership Setup

## Recommended initial structure

Form one private company limited by shares, registered in England and Wales. A working legal name is **Clubroom Technology Limited**, trading as **Clubroom**, subject to Companies House and UK Intellectual Property Office clearance.

Do not begin with a sole trade, partnership, community interest company, holding-company group, or multiple operating subsidiaries unless a qualified adviser identifies a concrete need. A single operating company is the simplest structure for contracts, intellectual property, investment, insurance, payroll, payment-provider onboarding, and accounts.

## Founder setup

Subject to solicitor and accountant confirmation:

- one founder, shareholder, director, and person with significant control;
- 100 fully paid ordinary shares with equal voting, dividend, and capital rights;
- model articles initially, unless financing or another shareholder is imminent;
- a board record approving incorporation, banking, contracts, providers, insurance, and intellectual-property acquisition;
- no informal promises of equity—use written option or share documents when needed;
- put a founder service agreement, salary/dividend policy, expenses policy, and director’s loan accounting in place with an accountant.

The exact nominal value, remuneration, and tax treatment must be set with an accountant. The share count is an administrative recommendation, not tax advice.

## Formation sequence

1. Search the Companies House register and UK trade mark register for **Clubroom**, close variants, logos, and relevant software/sports/marketplace classes.
2. Decide whether the legal name and trading name should differ.
3. Verify the founder’s identity for Companies House and obtain the required personal code.
4. Choose a registered office that protects the founder’s home address where appropriate and complies with Companies House rules.
5. Incorporate the company, identify the PSC, adopt articles, issue shares, and retain statutory records.
6. Open a company bank account. Never mix personal, operating-company, seller, or customer money.
7. Register Corporation Tax services, bookkeeping, payroll if applicable, and VAT when required or strategically chosen.
8. Register with the ICO and pay the data protection fee if required.
9. Assign all existing Clubroom code, designs, domains, documentation, databases, trade marks, social accounts, and other intellectual property from the founder to the company in writing.
10. Make every contractor and employee sign confidentiality and present-assignment IP terms before contributing.
11. Obtain insurance and professional sign-off before live users or money.

## Suggested SIC codes for adviser confirmation

- **63120 — Web portals** as the likely primary marketplace activity.
- **62012 — Business and domestic software development** as a secondary activity.
- Add **93199 — Other sports activities** only if Clubroom itself genuinely provides sports activities; using it casually could undermine the intended platform-only stance.

## Why not a CIC

Clubroom has a social and safeguarding purpose, but the stated objective is also a profitable, investable founder business. A CIC introduces an asset lock and restrictions that may conflict with conventional investment and returns. Social-purpose obligations should instead appear in product rules, governance, safety controls, transparent reporting, and contractual commitments unless the founder deliberately chooses an asset-locked mission.

## Territory and governing law

Use English law and the courts of England and Wales in platform and seller contracts, subject to mandatory consumer rights. A governing-law clause does not remove protections a consumer receives under mandatory law.

For a manageable launch, restrict sellers, venues, and delivered sessions to England until Scottish, Welsh sector-specific, Northern Irish, and international differences are separately reviewed. If Wales is included, obtain a Wales-specific safeguarding and operational review even though England and Wales share a legal jurisdiction.

## Future restructuring triggers

Reconsider the simple structure only when one of these happens:

- institutional investment requires bespoke articles, preference shares, or an option pool;
- a co-founder or material shareholder joins;
- regulated payment or financial activity cannot remain entirely within a licensed provider;
- material international operations create tax or regulatory exposure;
- valuable intellectual property needs a separately justified holding structure;
- Clubroom directly employs a coaching workforce or operates venues.

Do not create entities merely to “limit liability” when the same people, contracts, money, and operations remain mixed. Corporate separation works only when operational separation is real.
